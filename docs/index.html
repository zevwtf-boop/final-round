The scoring, explained +

Correct answers earn 500–1,000 points depending on how quickly the server receives them. The finale doubles that to 1,000–2,000. Wrong or missed answers earn zero. Points = round((1 − elapsed time ÷ time limit ÷ 2) × maximum points). Ties use number correct, then total response time on correct answers. Exact ties stay tied for host review.

Fair play and prizes +

Answers are shuffled, timings and scores are checked on the server, and invitations are single-use. Outside help and extra devices cannot be fully prevented. Your host announces the prize, eligibility and tie rules before play and handles delivery. Double points does not double the prize. No payment or Epic Games login is required.
