02 / THE GROUND RULES

Keep it a fair fight.

Know the game before the first question drops.

How do points work?+

A correct answer earns up to 1,000 points. Faster correct answers earn more: points = round((1 − response time ÷ question time ÷ 2) × 1,000). Wrong or missed answers earn zero. The final question uses 2,000 instead.

What keeps the game fair?+

Personal single-use codes, host approval, shuffled answer positions, server-set deadlines, and one locked submission per round. Correct answers stay private until the reveal. Outside help and extra devices can’t be reliably detected; play on your own.

How is the winner decided?+

Players rank by points, then correct answers, then fastest total response time. Exact ties require host review. Network delay affects speed scores. The host must announce prize value, eligibility and tie rules before starting.

What about the V-Bucks?+

The host provides and delivers the prize outside this site. The final round doubles quiz points, not the V-Bucks amount. No payment or Epic Games sign-in is required here. Never share your Epic password or verification codes.
